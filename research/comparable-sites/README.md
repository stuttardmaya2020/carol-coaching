## Key takeaways

- Lead with Carol's background: "former headhunter" positions her as quickly as "former recruiter" does for Hannah Salton.
- Make the first step concrete and free: "A coffee or a call, on me. No obligation." That's warmer than any competitor's chemistry call.
- Offer separate paths by career stage, attribute testimonials properly, and keep proof to one strip.
- Avoid superlatives, jargon, packages, pop-ups and logo walls.
- Build the mood board around Carol's black-and-white photography, which none of the competitors match.
