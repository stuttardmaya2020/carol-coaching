# Home

Scope: the Home route (`site/src/Home.jsx` and its components). Mode: Persuade. Code-led; no image generation in this project.

Audience and job: a referred visitor at any career stage, usually on a phone, deciding whether to get in touch. One action: "Let's have a coffee" (mailto to the placeholder address), with the phone placeholder beside it.

Proof: three real testimonials, transcribed from `research/current-site/screenshots/testimonials.png` (Alex Quinan for starting out, Amanda Jones for mid-career, Kerry Glazer for leaders); 20+ years, 3,000+ people, former headhunter, training; the Kathleen Saxton recognition, unnamed book. "As few as two sessions" comes from the old How coaching works page.

Constraints: portraits are labelled placeholders; no prices, packages or session lengths; no eyebrow labels; no stat-tile numbers.

Open: final stage-pitch wording (draft for Carol); whether an Alfie photo appears (not on Home for now).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is a short, signed letter from Carol. It refuses the coaching-site default of a headline, three service cards and a logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: white page and charcoal Newsreader set at letter size, one violet italic phrase per heading, lilac fields for quotes and the how-it-works band, an apricot highlight reserved for the line you jumped to, a deep violet close. Pill buttons, underlined violet links, no shadows or gradients.

STORY: the visitor meets Carol, picks the "If you're…" line that fits, reads that part of the letter with a peer's testimonial, sees how the free coffee leads to sessions, trusts her background, then writes to her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: small black-and-white portrait left (about a fifth of the width); the letter column right at roughly 30px serif; three underlined "If you're…" links; the italic "Carol" signature beside the violet coffee pill. On phones, the portrait sits small above the letter.

FORM: "A letter from Carol", position 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:ae797c3ed5315b5d. Signature interaction: each "If you're…" link scrolls to its section, whose heading repeats the line and draws an apricot highlight under it when it is the target.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
